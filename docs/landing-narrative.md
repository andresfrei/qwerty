# Guided public landing

The public `/` page is synchronous and does not read cookies, session or business data to choose its copy. The header always exposes a native `Ingresar` link to `/ingresar`; that separate private route resolves fresh identity. See [entry contract](landing-entry.md).

The landing keeps Qwerty's cream, ink and lime tokens and Manrope from the unchanged shared layout. A landing-only stylesheet scopes every selector beneath `.qwerty-landing`; it does not alter the global reset, Owner or Auth styles.

## One illustrative turn

On wide, sufficiently tall viewports, one example panel remains beside the story and updates when chapters enter the reading area. Its chapter links are ordinary anchors. The client observer is progressive enhancement, uses height-based pixel margins, rebuilds on resize, disconnects on unmount and performs no network requests. On each observer notification it reads current geometry of every observed chapter, filters valid stages intersecting the existing height-based reading band, then chooses the nearest top to the reading line. An exit-only notification can therefore select a still-visible chapter that emitted no entry. An empty band retains the latest selection. There is no scroll listener, animation-frame loop or scheduling queue.

At narrower widths the persistent panel is hidden and each chapter contains its own inline illustration. A separate compact paper receipt now sits inside the mobile hero: one example order, its illustrative amount, and distinct preparation, payment and settlement labels. It is not the full operations card squeezed smaller. Mobile spacing keeps the summary alongside the headline and CTA in the first 390×844 viewport. The actual normal/reduced-JS run observed its paper bounds at y501.69–668.19px, plus the 7px tear, inside the 844px viewport; see the partial browser evidence below. Short desktop windows do not pin the panel.

The latest explicit user correction replaces the green dashboard-card appearance with a printed-paper receipt, consistently across desktop, mobile inline examples and the compact hero. White paper, dark ink, receipt-local system monospace, dashed/dotted rules, small corners and subtle neutral depth distinguish the order from the Manrope narrative. A decorative tear extends 7px into reserved bottom margin without clipping the content or conceptual disclaimer. The desktop receipt is at most 460px wide. Headers, order status and payment rows have no solid green fill or colored dashboard pills; all amounts, states and disclaimers are unchanged. Forest purpose blocks and lime narrative progress/CTA remain outside the receipt. The former full-forest figure/header requirements are superseded, not retained alongside the paper requirement. The desktop example body is keyed by stage for a single 360ms entrance (10px travel, partial—not hidden—opacity); a persistent progress fill transitions to the selected fraction over 420ms. Rapid chapter selections replace the keyed body immediately and retarget the fill, without queued timers or scroll loops. The compact hero summary has a single entrance; CTA hover/press and chapter links have brief feedback. There is no perpetual motion or animation-dependent reveal. Keyed content contains no focusable controls; native chapter links keep focus outside it.

All five positive animation/transition rules and CTA hover/press travel are gated under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. Base/default content stays visible and static. The reduced-motion reset remains as a safeguard, but is not relied on to outrank more-specific component rules; reduced motion leaves the final selected content visible without entrance or progress interpolation. With no JavaScript or observer support, the desktop example stays at its initial stage while every chapter, inline example, native link and FAQ remains readable HTML. CSS entrances can run without JavaScript under normal motion; content never waits for hydration, an observer or an animation completion event. Mobile continues normal document scrolling.

The same example order is used throughout. Preparation and delivery never imply payment confirmation; the transfer remains pending and a courier run can still await settlement. Order examples, states and amounts are labelled conceptual. The landing does not claim that all illustrated operations are implemented, promise provider-fee-free service, publish a price, fabricate customer metrics or offer a marketplace/courier fleet.
